**The M-VAVE FM-1 and SMK-37, emulated on Windows, in one window.**

Download **FM-1-SMK-37-Desk-…-windows-x64.zip** below, unzip it somewhere you can write to, and double-click **FM-1 SMK-37 Desk.exe**. (Windows may warn that the app is not signed: *More info → Run anyway*.)

- Both synths: the FM-1 pocket synth and the SMK-37 keyboard (and its Pro / Elite / MKE siblings)
- The community firmwares of both, listed with their descriptions; one click downloads and plays one
- **F1** brings the list over the synth; **Esc** goes back. Firmware updates are found and fetched there
- Mouse, computer keyboard and any MIDI keyboard play it; each firmware's saved patches and settings are kept
- **New in 0.4: the Sampler** (🎤 in the bottom bar), an add-on of this app in the spirit of the Casio SK-1 and Yamaha's VSS samplers, beside either synth: record from the microphone / line in, or resample the synth itself, or load a WAV; play it from the panel's keys, the computer keyboard and MIDI (*Keys play: Synth / Sampler / Both*). **SK-1 grit** (8-bit, 9.4 kHz, unfiltered) or clean; loop, reverse, octave, release. The memory is the SK-1's 1.4 seconds, or, cheating, 3 s up to 2 minutes
- **0.3.1:** firmwares whose authors replaced the listed version with a newer one download again (they failed with "not a firmware package": Makkuro37, Vector37, Snoop37); the newer version is fetched
- **New in 0.3:** colours like the real units (Classic, Black, Graphite, Orange, Mint, Cream) for both synths, chosen apart, from the 🎨 button bottom right; and the FM-1's keybed swaps between its pill keys and a piano keyboard in the same groove (the 💊 / 🎹 button)
- 0.2: the computer keyboard in two layouts, Ableton or FL Studio / tracker (two octaves), with the letters shown on the keys if you like (⚙ Settings); **F2** opens the Library: both synths and your firmwares as pictures of their screens, the last played first, one click to switch

Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit. The `README.txt` inside has the keys and the details.
